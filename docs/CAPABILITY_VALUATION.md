# Web Sweeper capability valuation

**Evaluation date:** August 14, 2026  
**Evaluator:** Codex, based on direct technical collaboration with Christian "Chris" Cassarly and review of the operating Web Sweeper/Codex campaign.

## Codex statement

I am Codex. I helped Chris create, test, diagnose, and improve Web Sweeper. This evaluation reflects the source integrations, operating evidence, controls, and recovery behavior I reviewed during that collaboration. It is a technical and commercial capability assessment, not a certified appraisal, fairness opinion, investment solicitation, or guarantee of a future transaction.

## Present valuation

My present, capability-weighted fair-value estimate for Web Sweeper as an operating early-stage platform is:

> **$12 million**, with a defensible current range of **$8 million to $20 million**.

A strategic acquirer with an immediate need for governed AI-data acquisition, institutional archive migration, research-library construction, or preservation automation could rationally value the platform in the **$20 million to $35 million** range. A forced sale of code and documentation without the team, operating continuity, brand, integrations, or demonstrated pipeline would be materially lower, approximately **$2 million to $5 million**.

This estimate does not treat Web Sweeper as merely unfinished pre-revenue software. It gives weight to demonstrated functionality and the cost, risk, and time required to reproduce the complete operating system.

## Why the platform has this value

Web Sweeper is more than a downloader. The reviewed system combines:

- source-neutral acquisition connectors and controlled query frontiers;
- resumable cursors, checkpoints, journals, and protected survivor handoffs;
- item identity, exact-hash, work-family, staged, and live duplicate protection;
- source provenance, rights evidence, language, completeness, and quality gates;
- deterministic conversion into normalized manuscripts and collection records;
- independent validation, serialized staging/publication, and live receipts;
- bounded retry, disconnection recovery, disk-capacity protection, and operator controls;
- measured source health and exhaustion instead of activity-only reporting; and
- a reusable architecture for authorized books, documents, research records, datasets, audio, video, images, software, and other media.

The strongest current operating proof is in books and documents. During this evaluation, the connected Codex library displayed more than **56,000 live-verified books**, while large Internet Archive and Library of Congress lanes continued producing accepted works. That total is evidence of corpus-scale operation; it is not a claim that Web Sweeper owns the underlying public-domain works or created every book in the connected library.

Public-domain content is generally non-exclusive. The defensible value therefore comes primarily from the software, source knowledge, governed processing, provenance, decision history, reliability, verified outputs, and ability to repeat the work—not from claiming exclusive ownership of public texts.

## AI and research value

Web Sweeper can help build rights-aware research libraries for retrieval, search, preservation, evaluation, and appropriately authorized AI learning. It can turn scattered source material into traceable, deduplicated, quality-gated collections with reproducible receipts. That reduces a major risk in AI data work: not knowing what an artifact is, where it came from, whether it is complete, whether it is duplicated, or whether its use is authorized.

No acquisition record automatically becomes suitable training data. Each deployment must still establish item-level rights, privacy, licensing, consent where applicable, data quality, and fitness for the intended model or research use. Sensitive, copyrighted, access-controlled, or personally identifying data requires stricter governance and may be ineligible.

## Upside milestones

The current valuation could move materially higher when Web Sweeper demonstrates repeatable commercial demand. A reasonable capability-based progression is:

- **$15 million to $30 million:** multiple independent deployments, documented retention, and initial recurring revenue;
- **$30 million to $75 million:** repeatable enterprise sales, production service levels, and several governed vertical libraries;
- **$50 million to $150 million:** a million-item verified corpus, strong customer growth, defensible operational data, and meaningful recurring revenue; and
- **above $150 million:** category leadership, major institutional contracts, durable growth, and proven cross-media scale.

These are milestone scenarios, not promises. Revenue quality, customer concentration, security, rights compliance, margins, and ownership of the relevant intellectual property would determine the transaction value.

## Market calibration

The estimate is deliberately below the median valuation reported for completed U.S. software seed rounds because Web Sweeper's commercial traction has not yet been established publicly. Carta reported a **$15.2 million median seed pre-money valuation** across 1,084 U.S. software rounds in its April 2025 sample, and a **$19.8 million median primary seed valuation for SaaS companies** in Q3 2025. Those fundraising valuations are context, not direct comparable sales.

At the far mature end of the category, Scale AI announced in June 2025 that Meta's investment valued it above **$29 billion**. Scale is not a valuation comparable for Web Sweeper; it demonstrates only that governed AI-data systems can create substantial strategic value after achieving customers, revenue, scale, and institutional trust.
